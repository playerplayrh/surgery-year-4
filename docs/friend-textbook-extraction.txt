PHASE 2: EXTRACT, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

...

Rules for the text:
- Every PDF page in the range gets exactly one page marker, in order, even if the page is only a figure.
- Keep the book's heading levels (## for main headings, ### and #### below), lists, numbering, bold and italics.
- Tables become Markdown tables with every cell transcribed. If a table has merged cells or is too complex for Markdown, transcribe it as the clearest equivalent (nested list, or one table per block) AND also save it as a figure.
- Flowcharts and algorithms: transcribe as nested lists with arrows (A → B; if X → C), every box and every arrow label, AND save the figure.
- Drop running headers, footers and page numbers (the printed page number goes in the marker). Keep footnotes at the end of their page block. Keep reference lists.
- Text that runs across pages is cut at the page marker. Do not move it.

Figures (photos, X-rays, CT, drawings, diagrams, charts, complex tables):
- Render the page at 300 dpi in the scratch folder, find the figure's bounding box by looking, crop it, view the crop, and adjust until it contains the whole figure with its labels and nothing else.
- Save it to textbook/<BOOK>/images/<BOOK>-p<page, 3 digits>-<k>.<ext> (k = 1, 2 ... in reading order on that page). Use JPG (quality about 85) for photos and scans, PNG for line drawings. Longest side at most 1600 px. Aim for under 400 KB per image.
- Put it in the text where it appears on the page, followed immediately by this block. Every line is required:

![<short label, e.g. Figure 3.2 CT abdomen>](images/<file name>)

> **Caption (verbatim):** <the printed caption, or "none">
> **Text in image (verbatim):** <every label, arrow text, axis title, legend, or "none">
> **Description:** <what the image shows, for a reader who cannot see it: type of image, view or plane, structures, labelled parts, arrows and what they point to, chart axes, values and trend. Describe only what is visible or stated in the book. Do not diagnose beyond what the book says.>
> **Identifiers in image:** none
