# Reference keys

Use these keys in `references:` and in textbook-map files, followed by the section or page,
e.g. `board-review-2020 p.45`. Download links for most of these are in
`Survival Guide/Material Sx from Vchakarn77.txt` and `Survival Guide/Survival guide Yr. 4.txt`.

| key | what | notes from the guides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `chula-black-1`, `chula-black-2` | Chula Surgery 1 and 2 ("เล่มดำ"), written by the department | Covers general and subspecialty. Exam answers are said to be referenced from it. Older and harder to read (P'Prung). Good for chapters with little lecture, e.g. vascular |
| `board-review-2020` | Surgical Board Review 2020 (Thai, Dr. Pongsanti) | Slide-style summary, easy and quick. Very detailed by disease; use judgment. Drive link in `sources/README.md` |
| `washington-manual` | The Washington Manual of Surgery | English summary with quizzes at the end of each chapter |
| `tokyo-guideline` | Tokyo Guidelines summary, clips by a Ramathibodi surgery attending | Biliary infection. Playlist and slides linked in the Vchakarn77 file |
| `schwartz` | Schwartz's Principles of Surgery | P'Prung rarely used it; some attendings assign readings |
| `nl2-2024` | NL2 review 2024, Dr. Pongsanti (slides and clips) | Key points only; covers general and subspecialty |
| `surgeonvivor` | Facebook page "surgeonvivor" | Shortened guidelines, easy to read |
| `subspe-note` | Subspe Sx Note.pdf (senior summary) | **Subspecialty, out of scope for now.** Drive link in `sources/README.md` |
