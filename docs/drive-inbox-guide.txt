HOW TO SEND MATERIAL TO OUR SURGERY STUDY REPO (no GitHub needed)
===================================================================

You only need the shared Google Drive folder "Surgery Group".

1. REMOVE PATIENT DETAILS FIRST
   No patient names, HN/AN, birth dates, exact dates, ward/bed numbers, phone numbers,
   and no photos showing faces, name labels or ID bands.
   Names of attendings and residents are fine.
   (Use age and sex only, e.g. "60s F", and the week of rotation, e.g. "W2".)

2. WRITE ONE LINE AT THE TOP saying what it is, for example:
     G3 · bedside facts · cholecystitis
     G1 · case · RUQ pain with jaundice
     Exam recall · 2026 Gen MCQ · answers recalled (not official)

3. UPLOAD IT TO THE FOLDER "inbox"
   Name the file:   G3 - cholecystitis - YourName.txt
   Accepted: a Google Doc, .txt, .docx, .pdf, or a clear photo of your notes.
   Messy notes are fine. Thai or English, as you like. Nothing is rewritten in your words.

4. CHECK BACK LATER
   - Moved to "done"       = it is in the repo now. Thank you!
   - Moved to "needs-fix"  = read the "NEEDS FIX" note next to your file, fix it, and
                             upload it to "inbox" again.

TO STUDY
   The folder "repo-copy" is a read-only copy of everything in the repo. Download the chapters or
   notes you need and upload them to a Project on claude.ai, then ask Claude to explain or quiz
   you. Everything is marked "ai-generated" (unchecked) until a second person verifies it.

TO VERIFY SOMETHING
   If you checked a file against the textbook or an attending, tell the group admin which file and
   what you checked. Your name is added as the verifier.
