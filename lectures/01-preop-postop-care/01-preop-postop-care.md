### NUTRITIONAL STATUS
- Poor Nutritional Status : **Significant weight loss – 5% in 1 month , 10% in 6 months**
  - Poor wound healing

  - Risk of infection

<!-- p.10 -->
### Physical examination
- Vital sign : **BP , heart rate , respiratory rate , oxygen saturation**
- Weight , Height → **BMI : obese , malnutrition**
- Cardiovascular : **heart sound , murmur, rhythm, peripheral edema**
- Respiratory : **respiratory rate and effort , breath sound , oxygen saturation**
- Gastrointestinal : **jaundice , abdominal mass ,liver and spleen size, ascites**
- Neurological : **consciousness level, muscle power**
- Airway assessment : **Mallampati classification**

### COAGULATION
- **aPTT ,PT , Platelet count**
- **Risk of bleeding**
  - Family history / history of abnormal bleeding
  - Liver disease(alcohol use), renal disease
  - Medication : antiplatelet , anticoagulation
  - Neuraxial anesthesia, Intracranial neurosurgery, major surgery
- **Preoperative dialysis in ESRD patient** : prevent uremic bleeding
- **Clinically significant thrombocytopenia** : < 50,000
